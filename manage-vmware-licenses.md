# Managing VMware licenses
{: #manage-vmware-licenses}

1. Log in to the [{{site.data.keyword.cloud_notm}} catalog](https://cloud.ibm.com/catalog/){: external}.
1. Go to **Devices > Manage > VMware&reg; licenses**. Each VMware&reg; add-on that is purchased displays the following information:

   * License key
   * CPUs
   * Order date

For more information about managing VMware licenses, see [Licensing for ESXi Hosts](https://techdocs.broadcom.com/us/en/vmware-cis/vsphere/vsphere/7-0/vsphere-single-host-management-vmware-host-client/host-management-with-the-vmware-host-client-vSphereSingleHostManagementVMwareHostClient/licensing-for-esxi-hosts-vSphereSingleHostManagementVMwareHostClient.html){: external}.

## Ordering VMware licenses
{: #ordering-vmware-licenses}

1. Log in to the [{{site.data.keyword.cloud_notm}} catalog](https://cloud.ibm.com/catalog/){: external}.
1. Go to **Devices > Manage > VMware licenses** and click **Order VMware licenses**.
1. Select the appropriate licenses and click **Continue**.
1. Review charges and the MSA and click **Place order**.

## Removing VMware licenses
{: #removing-vmware-licenses}

1. Log in to the [{{site.data.keyword.cloud_notm}} catalog](https://cloud.ibm.com/catalog/){: external}.
1. Go to **Devices > Manage > VMware licenses** and click **Remove license**. 
1. Click **Ok** to confirm.
